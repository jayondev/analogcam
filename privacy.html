Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Your photos never leave your device

Analogcam Lab processes every photo entirely inside your web browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. There is no server-side upload, no file storage, and no processing pipeline outside your device. When you close the tab, everything is gone — nothing was ever transmitted or retained anywhere.

2. Information we do collect

Analogcam Lab does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, or photos.

3. Third-party resources

This page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Loading a font involves a request to Google's servers, which may log standard technical information such as your IP address, in line with Google's own privacy policy. If we add advertising or analytics in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly and — where required by law (for example under GDPR for EEA/UK visitors) — a consent mechanism will be added before any such service loads.

4. Cookies

Analogcam Lab itself does not set cookies. Third-party resources (such as Google Fonts) may set minimal technical cookies as part of standard web delivery.

5. Children's privacy

Analogcam Lab is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, consistent with the fact that we do not collect personal information from any user.

6. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored on our servers, there is generally nothing to access, correct, or delete on our end. If you have questions about how this applies to you under GDPR, CCPA, or another regional law, please contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
